# Mint Woodland Pet marketing banner

`mint-woodland-pet-banner.png` is the wide reusable marketing banner. It is the
GitHub README hero and is also intended for external project promotion.

It uses the active Mint character reference for identity and combines two saved
visual directions:

- `../reference/poster/morning-pixel-art-reference.png` supplied the bright,
  welcoming sunrise mood and woodland pixel-art atmosphere.
- `../reference/poster/pixel-title-reference.png` supplied the bold retro title
  direction and character-left/title-right poster structure.

The final composition keeps Mint on the left, happily and curiously looking
toward the exact title **MINT WOODLAND PET** on the right. Mint retains the leaf
ears, peach flower, glossy plum eyes, cream belly, mint body, and curled fern
tail from `../reference/mint-woodland-pet-original.png`. A soft shadow anchors the
character to the meadow.

The poster was refined in two passes: the first established the composition and
title; the second adjusted Mint's pupils, head tilt, and upper-body direction to
make the rightward wondering look clear.
